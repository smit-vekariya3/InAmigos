Who We Are

A Movement Built on Compassion

2020 Founded

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). Based in Chhattisgarh, we operate across India addressing critical societal challenges through a network of dedicated professionals and volunteers.

We believe every act of kindness, however small, can ignite lasting change. From distributing meals to planting trees, from educating children to empowering women — every initiative reflects our commitment to a compassionate, inclusive, and sustainable society.

✔ Section 8 Non-Profit ✔ 80G & 12A Certified ✔ CSR-1 Registered ✔ NITI Aayog Registered ✔ IAF ISO 9001:2015

Our Initiatives

Six Projects. One Mission.

Every rupee raised is invested into transformative projects spanning education, food security, animal welfare, women's empowerment, skill development, and environmental sustainability.

Food & Clothing
Project SEVA

Tackling hunger and clothing poverty among impoverished communities. Beyond basic needs, it fosters dignity and ensures no family goes to bed hungry.

50K+ meals & clothes distributed
Education
Project BACHPANSHALA

Breaking the cycle of poverty through quality education — offering underprivileged children learning sessions, school supplies, digital literacy, and life skills.

1000s children supported
Women Empowerment
Project UDAAN

Rewriting stories of hope for women in rural India — providing vocational training, skill development, and pathways to financial independence and community leadership.

900+ girls empowered
Environment
Project PRAKRITI

Focused on sustainability through tree-planting drives, clean-up campaigns, and conservation workshops to instill environmental responsibility.

20K+ trees planted
Animal Welfare
Project JEEV

Extending compassion to all living beings — feeding and caring for stray animals and promoting humane treatment across communities every single day.

50+ animals fed daily
Skill Development
Project VIKAS

Bridging the employment gap through internships in data operations, finance, research, content writing, digital marketing, and social work.

30K+ interns trained in 4 years

Our Story

The InAmigos Journey

September 2020
Foundation Established

Mr. Govind Shukla founded InAmigos Foundation on September 23, 2020 in Chhattisgarh with a vision to create lasting social impact across India.

2020 – 2021
Project SEVA Launches

The first initiative — food and clothing distribution — begins, reaching thousands of underprivileged families in Chhattisgarh and neighbouring regions.

2021 – 2022
Education & Empowerment Expands

Projects BACHPANSHALA and UDAAN take shape, delivering digital literacy to children and vocational training to women across rural India.

2022 – 2023
VIKAS, PRAKRITI & JEEV Launch

Internship programs, environmental drives, and animal welfare initiatives are introduced, creating a 360° impact model addressing all dimensions of society.

2024
30,000+ Interns Trained

Project VIKAS crosses a landmark milestone — 30,000+ young professionals trained in four years, strengthening India's skill ecosystem from the grassroots.

2025 – Present
Growing Nationwide

With ISO 9001:2015, NITI Aayog, and CSR-1 certifications, InAmigos Foundation continues expanding its reach and inspiring collective action across India.
